## Current state

`/content-review` now receives one validated teacher-facing document from a
server-only adapter. Copy, section order, and the six local product screens no
longer come from separate client-side graphs. Reviewer pins and rationale remain
separate from the teacher document. Stable content IDs, review references,

## Decisions to preserve

- Do not modify `src/routes/index.tsx`, `src/routes/__root.tsx`,
  `src/content/landing.ts`, or current public landing components until cutover is
  explicitly proven safe.
- Keep teacher content and reviewer rationale as separate documents/overlays.
- Never use filenames, array indexes, byte offsets, or visible labels as durable
  CMS identities. The current adapter pairs story screens by server-only semantic
  bindings; the database model must replace these with immutable target IDs.
- Keep section layouts bounded. This is a CMS for approved page templates, not a
  free-form website builder.
- Do not claim that Save, undo, version history, restore, publishing, shared-link
  capability exchange, or durable comments exist yet. Those are later phases.
