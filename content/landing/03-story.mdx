# One student, carried through every moment

The landing page follows one synthetic student's care journey, from the first sign of eligibility to a family in the loop and a record kept. No real student appears on this page.

<Item id="promise" label="A student in your class">

## Xiao Ming is one of thirty-two students you teach.

His family's circumstances changed last term. The signs sit in separate systems, and none of them raises a hand.

</Item>

<Item id="notice" label="You notice">

## Xiao Ming's family may qualify for support. Nobody has applied.

A sibling already receiving financial assistance. Household circumstances updated last term. No bursary active on file. One profile showed it all, before the application window closed, not after.

</Item>

<Item id="next-steps" label="You know the steps">

## The right scheme finds you.

Which bursary fits his situation, what documents the family needs, where to submit, and when it closes, surfaced on his profile. No circular-hunting. No asking down the corridor. The process by the book, without opening the book.

</Item>

<Item id="words" label="The words are ready">

## A clear message, already drafted.

Telling a family they may qualify for support is a delicate note to write. The draft starts from his context, in your tone, with the steps and documents laid out plainly. You review and edit every word before anything is sent.

</Item>

<Item id="family-and-record" label="The family is in the loop. So is the record.">

## Sent. Seen. On file.

Delivered to the family, response tracked, logged on the student's record. When the application goes in, you'll know. When it's approved, so will the record.

</Item>
