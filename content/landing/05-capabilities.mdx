# The apps, up close

For the visitor who now wants specifics. Each capability in journey order: the job it does, and one concrete school scenario.

<Item id="student-insights" label="Student Insights">

## Every signal about a student, in one profile.

A form teacher opens one profile and sees attendance, wellbeing notes, and family context together, including a sibling already receiving financial assistance and no bursary on file.

</Item>

<Item id="next-step" label="AI next-step guidance">

## The right process for this case, surfaced where you are.

A student may qualify for a bursary. The matching scheme, the documents the family needs, where to submit, and when it closes appear on his profile. No handbook required.

</Item>

<Item id="message-drafting" label="Message drafting">

## A first draft from the student's context, for you to review and edit.

A delicate note telling a family they may qualify for support starts drafted in your tone, with the steps laid out. You edit every word before it goes anywhere.

</Item>

<Item id="posts" label="Posts">

## Reach the family and keep the communication on record.

The message reaches the family in minutes, the response is tracked, and the whole exchange lands on the student's communication record.

</Item>
