# Four ways to move everyday work forward

Teacher Workspace brings related capabilities into one product. Each capability supports a distinct part of a teacher's work.

<Item id="student-insights" label="Student Insights">

## Notice and understand changes around a student.

Review the student information available to you and recognise patterns worth following up.

</Item>

<Item id="next-step" label="AI next-step guidance">

## Review a possible next step.

Consider guidance for the task in front of you before deciding what fits.

</Item>

<Item id="message-drafting" label="Message drafting">

## Prepare an editable first draft.

Start from a draft, then review and edit every word before sharing it.

</Item>

<Item id="posts" label="Posts">

## Communicate with families.

Publish an approved message and check the delivery information available to you.

</Item>
