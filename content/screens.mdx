<Item id="hero">

## Connected positive-growth view

Help teachers see what is going well and decide what to do next for a synthetic class student. Keep recent observations, a possible next step, and a family message together.

- Positive growth · Growing confidence tag
- Recent observation summary
- Next step, message draft, and Posts

</Item>

<Item id="story-promise">

## Positive student profile

Help teachers recognise a student’s progress at a glance. Use a synthetic student from the general class list, highlight the positive-growth tag, and show no additional-needs or attention tag.

- Synthetic student and class
- Positive growth · Growing confidence tag
- Latest teacher observation

</Item>

<Item id="story-notice">

## Evidence behind the positive tag

Make the positive tag traceable to recent classroom evidence. Show a short timeline of notes that makes the student’s growing confidence easy to recognise, without risk or support indicators.

- Two or three synthetic observations
- Positive tag remains visible
- Teacher note source labels

</Item>

<Item id="story-next-steps">

## Teacher-controlled next-step guidance

Help the teacher turn positive evidence into a small next step. Suggest one low-stakes way to invite another contribution, with the supporting observations beside it for review.

- Suggested action
- Why it may help
- Review or adapt state

</Item>

<Item id="story-words">

## Editable family message

Help the teacher prepare a family update without losing control of the wording. Show a short draft built from the positive evidence and mark it as editable and awaiting review.

- Draft family update
- Supporting context
- Review and edit state

</Item>

<Item id="story-family-and-record">

## Posts preview and student record

Help the teacher check what will be shared and where it will be recorded. Show the reviewed family update beside its place in the student record and keep the preview visibly unsent.

- Posts preview
- Family audience
- Student communication timeline

</Item>

<Item id="explorer-choose">

## Positive scenario picker

Let teachers start with a constructive classroom moment. Offer only synthetic examples and preselect “Contributing more in class” for this walkthrough.

- Selected positive scenario
- Synthetic example marker
- Capabilities included

</Item>

<Item id="explorer-context">

## Connected student context

Help teachers review the evidence and next step together. Bring the positive tag, supporting observations, and possible next step into one view.

- Positive tag
- Observation timeline
- Suggested next step

</Item>

<Item id="explorer-action">

## Teacher review before sharing

Let teachers keep control of what reaches a family. Show the editable message and Posts preview together, and keep both unsent until the teacher approves them.

- Editable message
- Posts preview
- Preview-only status

</Item>
