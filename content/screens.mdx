<Item id="hero">

## Focused product peek at the fold

Show one approved Teacher Workspace state rather than a fictional home screen. The crop should make one everyday teacher task understandable within seconds.

- One approved capability and task
- Synthetic, non-sensitive information only
- Enough interface context to identify the product location

</Item>

<Item id="story-promise">

## Student Insights class view with a positive tag

Highlight one synthetic student whose recent participation is improving. The positive tag must be the focal point, with no conduct, risk, or deficit framing.

- Positive tag such as “Contributing with confidence”
- Student row and class context
- Route to recent positive observations

</Item>

<Item id="story-notice">

## Positive observation detail

Show the recent observations behind the positive tag. Dates and short notes help the teacher understand the change without turning the student into a case.

- Two or three positive classroom observations
- Clear dates or sequence
- Teacher-owned interpretation and follow-up

</Item>

<Item id="story-next-steps">

## AI next-step guidance for a separate task

Use a separate synthetic classroom-planning moment so the page does not imply an automatic hand-off from Student Insights. Present one possible next step for teacher review.

- Short description of the teacher's task
- One suggested next step with its basis
- Visible choice to use, adapt, or leave the suggestion

</Item>

<Item id="story-words">

## Message draft for a positive class update

Show a separate positive class moment in an editable draft. The interface must make clear that the teacher reviews the message before it is shared.

- Positive class-update subject and body
- Editing controls and unsent status
- Explicit teacher review before publishing

</Item>

<Item id="story-family-and-record">

## Posts delivery overview

Show the same class update after the teacher publishes it through Posts. Include only the delivery information approved for public GA copy.

- Published message status
- Approved delivery or read information
- Route back to the sent post for follow-up

</Item>
