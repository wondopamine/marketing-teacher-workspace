<Item id="hero">

## Connected care view

Help teachers see one student's full picture and act on it. Keep the eligibility signal, the right next step, and the family message together in one place.

- Synthetic student profile
- Support eligibility prompt
- Next step and drafted message

</Item>

<Item id="story-promise">

## Class view with one synthetic student

Ground the story in an ordinary class. Show Xiao Ming as one row among many, with no conduct, offence, or attention marker anywhere in the frame.

- Synthetic class list
- Xiao Ming's profile entry
- No conduct or attention markers

</Item>

<Item id="story-notice">

## Eligibility signals in one profile

Make the "may qualify" claim traceable. Show the three signals side by side so the teacher's noticing is believable, with nothing about the student's own behaviour.

- Sibling's financial-assistance record
- Household circumstances update
- No active bursary on file

</Item>

<Item id="story-next-steps">

## Scheme guidance on the profile

Show the process arriving where the teacher already is. The matched scheme and its steps sit beside the student context that triggered them.

- Matched support scheme
- Documents the family needs
- Where to submit and when it closes

</Item>

<Item id="story-words">

## Editable family message

Keep the teacher in control of a delicate note. Show the draft built from the student's context, visibly editable and unsent.

- Draft family message
- Steps and documents laid out
- Review and edit state

</Item>

<Item id="story-family-and-record">

## Delivery and record view

Close the loop in one view. Show the sent message, the family's response, and the same exchange on the student's record.

- Sent and seen status
- Family response tracking
- Student communication timeline

</Item>
