## Post composer with the draft

Keep the teacher in control of a delicate note. The Posts composer holds the draft built from the student's context, in the rich-text editor, visibly editable and not yet sent.

- Draft family message in the composer
- Steps and documents laid out in the draft body
- Editable, unsent state before the send confirmation

</Item>

Close the loop on the Posts screens. The sent post carries its posted status, and the recipient read table shows the family has seen it.

- Recipient read table with the family marked as read
- The exchange kept on the student's record
