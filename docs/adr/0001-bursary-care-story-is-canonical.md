# The bursary care story is canonical again

The GA landing page tells issue #3's bursary near-miss story, synthetic student "Xiao Ming", family eligibility spotted before the application window closed, as the canonical five-act narrative. This reverses the 2026-08-04 brainstorm decision that made the positive-growth story canonical and the financial-assistance story non-canonical.

The reversal exists because the original substitution resolved a **data** problem as a **narrative** problem. The real blocker was the ticket's screenshot containing real-looking student names: a publication rule about one asset, which synthetic content already solves. The brainstorm's R9 bans deficit-framed stories (bullying, discipline, crisis) and requires framing through "growth, opportunity, **support**, and potential"; a support-eligibility story is on the allowed list. Meanwhile the growth story structurally could not carry the PM's stated angle, a near-miss averted ("you spotted it before the window closed, not after"), because nothing in it was at risk.

## Considered options

- **Keep the growth story**: Rejected. It reduces the page's claim to "nice, and slightly faster"; the PM's value-position angle disappears.
- **Switch the vehicle to attendance** (the MOE guided-limit near-miss from the same problem statement): Rejected after checking product fixtures. Declining attendance is coupled to offences, counselling, and poor conduct throughout `student-insights-app/data.ts`, so the story student reads as "the troubled kid", and "absent without valid reason, approaching the limit" is deficit framing, exactly what R9 bans. Bursary carries more sensitive data with positive framing; the synthetic-data rule governs the data, R9 governs the framing.

## Guardrails (bind every rendering of the story)

- The student is purpose-built synthetic ("Xiao Ming", deliberately generic so it reads as illustrative), never taken from the product demo fixtures.
- No attention tag, conduct grade, offence count, or counselling marker appears on the student anywhere in the story or its interface briefs. Eligibility signals come from household context and a sibling's record, never the student's own behaviour.
- Public copy spells out "financial assistance"; the FAS abbreviation stays denylisted in the review projection.
- The ticket's bursary screenshot (real-looking names) remains unpublishable; its comment URL stays denylisted in build output.
- "Xiao Ming" and "bursary" were removed from the projection and build denylists to admit the story; the internal-name bans (Contextual Intelligence, HeyTalia, SwAN) are unchanged.

## Consequences

Act 2's three eligibility signals and Act 3's scheme-guidance scope are load-bearing product claims that Student Insights and AI next-step guidance must actually support. The PM confirmed both on 2026-08-07 (signals as written; guidance includes closing dates), relayed by the Designer. Every act still stays `proposed` until product-claims approval is formally recorded in the governance model.
