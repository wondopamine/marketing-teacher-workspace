# Use three positive, GA-verified scenarios

Status: Accepted on 2026-08-13. Supersedes ADR-0001.

The GA landing page will use three separate positive, synthetic scenarios:
notice + understand, act, and communicate. Each scenario is selected only
after the relevant capability owner confirms the GA claims it demonstrates.

The page will not carry one student through a fictional integrated workflow.
Teacher Workspace contains related capabilities, but the landing page must not
invent automatic hand-offs between them.

## Why

The existing bursary near-miss depends on claims that shared-CMS reviewers
challenged: circular reading, automatic scheme matching, application outcome
tracking, and drafting from student context. It also narrows the platform to a
sensitive support case.

Positive, separate scenarios widen the story to everyday teachers and are more
suitable for public clearance. They let each capability demonstrate one
verified job without borrowing credibility from another capability.

## Guardrails

- Use purpose-built synthetic data only.
- Show progress, opportunity, or a constructive everyday moment.
- Keep the teacher as the decision-maker.
- Exclude real data, SWaN framing, behavioural-risk narratives, and unsupported
  cross-capability hand-offs.
- Do not publish any scenario until its described behaviour appears in the GA
  claims register with evidence and an approver.

## Consequences

- The Xiao Ming bursary story remains historical review material. It is not the
  canonical direction for the next CMS candidate.
- Existing CMS comments remain attached to their current stable targets until
  an append-only draft replaces the copy.
- The shared CMS draft does not change until the claims, hero screen, scenarios,
  school assurance, Posts proof, and measurement contract are approved.
